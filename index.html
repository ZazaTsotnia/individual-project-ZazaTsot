History Live Set My Sound

THE DETROIT LEGACY

Detroit is where it all started. Back in the mid 80s, a few geniuses, the Belleville Three, fused machine-like beats with soulful grooves and basically invented techno. It’s raw, futuristic, and still hits different today.

Underground techno exists because this music was never meant for the mainstream. It was a movement for people who wanted something different, something deeper, something that made you feel like you were part of a secret world. Every time I spin or produce, I feel that Detroit energy pulsing through my tracks.

TECHNO ACROSS THE GLOBE

After Detroit, techno did not stay in the US. It crossed the Atlantic and found a home in Europe where it became a lifeline for the LGBTQ+ underground scene. Amid war, oppression, and discrimination, techno offered a safe space, a place where people could just be themselves. Today that energy still lives on. Whether you are getting lost in the rhythm on the dance floor or producing and mixing your own tracks, techno remains a world where expression has no limits.

Techno has reached Georgia, Tbilisi, where the underground scene is flourishing. There are countless producers and venues shaping a vibrant community, putting Tbilisi on the map as a city that truly embraces the spirit of underground techno.
